# filmcraft

**Free online video editor, color & audio tools**

🌐 Website: [filmcraft](https://filmcraft.tools/)

## About

filmcraft is a free online video editor that runs in the browser. You can import clips, refine color and audio, work with motion and titles, and preview without creating an account. After verified sign-in, supported projects and exports can be saved privately with zero generation credits—so everyday cuts do not require a paid plan.

AI image and video generation is available in the same product family but uses credits separately from the free editing path. Credit pricing follows the shared plan ladder: monthly Lite $29.90 (600 credits), Standard $49.90 (1,500), Pro $99.90 (3,600), Max $199.90 (8,000), yearly billing at about 50% less, and one-time credit packs. Annual plans advertise roughly half the monthly cost while each generation still spends the same credits for the same settings.

The editor is aimed at people who need lightweight timeline work—trim, color, audio, titles—without installing desktop software, and who may optionally reach for credit-based AI tools later. Manual editing and free export after sign-in are the core promise; AI remains an add-on you open only when you are missing footage a timeline cannot invent.

## Features

- Free browser timeline editing after verified sign-in
- Color, audio, motion and title tools
- Import, edit and preview without an account
- Save supported projects and exports privately with zero credits
- Separate AI video and image generation with credits
- No desktop install required
